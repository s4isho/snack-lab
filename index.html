CHURFRIES

Sweet meets savoury — and you can’t tell which is which.

Bite-sized churros and crispy fries that look identical. Made from all-purpose flour, butter, milk, sugar, egg, potatoes, cornstarch, cheese, and salt. Served hot with a rich chocolate dip (melted chocolate, milk, water, sugar).

SHAWBURG

Shawarma flavours, burger convenience.

A pita wrap filled with seasoned beef, garlic mayonnaise, cheese sauce, cucumber, tomatoes, and onions. Two pieces per cup — neat, portable, and satisfying. A creative twist on two familiar favourites.

UBENA‑CHEE

Sweet banana. Rich ube. Savoury cheese.

Bite-sized banana pieces coated in a crispy golden layer, filled with a ube-and-cheese mixture. Soft, flavourful filling with a satisfying crunch — a unique snack for school breaks and beyond.

◂ Why We Made These ▸

FAMILIAR. SURPRISING. SATISFYING.

We take the snacks people already love — churros, fries, shawarma, burgers, banana cue, ube, cheese — and rearrange them into something new. Every product is designed to be affordable, convenient, and genuinely fun to eat. No gimmicks. Just thoughtful combinations that deliver on flavour and texture.
